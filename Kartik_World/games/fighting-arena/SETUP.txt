FIGHTING ARENA — GRAPHICS UPDATE

This is the 3D fighting game for Kartik World.

Visual upgrades:
- Detailed 3D fighters with armor, helmets, gloves and boots
- Animated idle stance and walking/running movement
- Punch and special-strike animations
- Glowing arena floor, neon ring, pillars and lighting
- Hit sparks, impact particles, shockwave and camera shake
- Enemy hit reactions and wave progression

Controls:
PC:
- W A S D or Arrow Keys: Move
- SPACE: Punch
- SHIFT: Special strike
- R: Restart

Mobile:
- Use the on-screen directional pad
- PUNCH: Normal attack
- SPECIAL: Strong area strike

Run the main website with VS Code Live Server and open Games > Fighting Arena.
Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr CDN, so an internet connection is required.
